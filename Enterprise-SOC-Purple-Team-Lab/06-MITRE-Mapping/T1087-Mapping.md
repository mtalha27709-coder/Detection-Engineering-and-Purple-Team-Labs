# T1087 - Account Discovery

## MITRE Overview

T1087 describes the process of identifying user accounts available on a compromised system or network. Attackers perform account discovery to identify privileged users and plan future stages of an attack.

## Purple Team Activity

Account enumeration activities were performed within the monitored laboratory environment.
